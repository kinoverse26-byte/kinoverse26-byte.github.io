Welcome

A café-restaurant for every hour of the day

Café du Stade is a café-restaurant at 40 Route de Veyrier in Carouge, Geneva. On weekdays the doors open at six in the morning and stay open until midnight. At the weekend, the day starts at eleven and runs until two in the morning.

Come by for an early coffee, lunch or a late evening with friends. When the weather is good, the terrace has seats for 40 guests.
