Welcome to Queen of Sheba

The warmth of Berbere.
The aroma of Alicha.

Two distinct seasonings shape the menu. Berbere brings warmth to the spicy dishes, prepared at a medium spice level. Aromatic Alicha offers a milder choice.

Build a vegetarian combination, choose sizzling mushrooms or explore beef, lamb and chicken dishes. Injera brings the meal together.

A note about injera & dietary needs

The restaurant’s regular injera includes barley and may contain other gluten-containing flours. For 100% teff injera, call ahead; an additional $3 per person may apply. Please discuss allergies, ingredients and preparation directly with the restaurant.

Prices shown are from the supplied published menu and are subject to confirmation. Meat prices may change with market conditions. Call +1-503-287-6302 for current details.
