Welcome to Bedouin

A hidden gem in Karen

Tucked away on Langata Road in Karen, Bedouin Restaurant serves Middle Eastern cooking in a relaxed, cosy setting. Guests remember the food as unique and the welcome as gracious.

Breakfast, seafood and prawns are the dishes people mention most. Call ahead to book a table, ask about today's menu, or plan a meal with family and friends.
