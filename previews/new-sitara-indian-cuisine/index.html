About us

Welcome to New Sitara

New Sitara Indian Cuisine is the Indian restaurant at 1779 Wells Branch Parkway, Austin. "Sitara" means star, and the cooking is what we want you to remember: warming spices, rich sauces and bread to wipe the plate clean.

Whether it's a family dinner or food for the office, the quickest way to us is the phone. Call to place your order or ask what's cooking today.
