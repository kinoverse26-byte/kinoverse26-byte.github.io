Welcome to Le Colibri

A neighbourhood restaurant in Geneva

Le Colibri sits at 68 Rue de Carouge, at street level, in the heart of Geneva. Drop in early on your way to work, settle in at midday, or come back in the evening with friends.

Our menu and prices are available on request - simply call us and we'll be happy to tell you what's on today.
