About us

A restaurant named after the sound of fire

You'll find us on Ze'ev Jabotinsky Street in Bnei Brak, at number 100 – one of the city's main roads, a short ride from central Tel Aviv.

The best way to know what's cooking today, and to save your table, is simply to call us or send a WhatsApp message. We'll be glad to tell you about the menu.
