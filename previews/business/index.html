The venue

A brand-new building, designed for celebrations

Enastros is a reception and events hall created by the Meimaridis brothers. It is housed in a brand-new building that was studied and built specifically to host receptions – not adapted from something else.

Its position in the middle of Thessaloniki's ring road (Περιφερειακή) makes it, in the family's words, the most easily accessible hall in the city for your guests – whether they come from the eastern side, the western side, or from outside Thessaloniki.
