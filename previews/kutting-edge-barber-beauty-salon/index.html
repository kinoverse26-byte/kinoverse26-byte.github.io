About the salon

A barber chair and a beauty chair, under one roof in Wells Branch.

Kutting Edge Barber & Beauty Salon is a neighborhood spot in Unit 110A on Wells Branch Parkway, North Austin. Barber and beauty services share the same welcoming room, so the whole family can come in together.

No appointment? No problem: walk-ins are welcome. Prefer a set time? Give us a call and ask what works for you.
