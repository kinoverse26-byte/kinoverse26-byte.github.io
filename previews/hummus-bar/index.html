About us

Your hummus address in central Limassol.

Hummus Bar is a restaurant at 23 Gladstonos, 3041 Limassol. The name says what we are about, and the table is open to everyone: vegan and vegetarian guests will find options made for them.

Drop in from 10:00 for a late breakfast or lunch, or come in the evening. We stay open until 23:00 from Tuesday to Sunday, and guests have free Wi-Fi.
