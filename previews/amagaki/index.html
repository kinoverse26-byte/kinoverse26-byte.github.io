About AMAGAKI

A Japanese table in the heart of Geneva

甘柿 · amagaki

In Japanese, amagaki is the sweet persimmon. On Rue Jean-Violette, it is the name of a Japanese restaurant open for lunch on weekdays and for dinner six evenings a week.

Whether you drop by at noon or come for a late evening at the weekend, the simplest way to secure your table is a quick call. Menu, specialities and prices are available on request.

6 eveningsDinner from 18:00, Monday to Saturday
