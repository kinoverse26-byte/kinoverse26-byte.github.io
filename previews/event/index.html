About Event Hall

Lasting touches for the most beautiful event of your life

“When your gaze is lost in the waters of the Thermaic Gulf and rests on the peaks of Olympus, you are surely on the green slopes of Event Hall.”

Couples come to us for our many years of experience in organising weddings and for the high quality of our services. We offer three distinctive reception halls designed to impress you and every one of your guests.

Choose our venue and our experienced team will take care of all your requirements – turning the reception of your dreams into reality.
