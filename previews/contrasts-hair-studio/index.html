Welcome to Contrasts

Great hair, without the harsh chemicals

As a local Brisbane organic hair salon, Contrasts Hair Studio is committed to delivering an exceptional hair salon experience without endangering your health or the environment.

We have made a conscious effort to select only products that are completely ammonia free, sulphate free and paraben free. That makes them environmentally friendly and safe for even the most sensitive of skin, without sacrificing results.

We pride ourselves on quality cutting, colouring and styling, and we understand the desire to look your best. Every client is welcomed with a complimentary consultation.
