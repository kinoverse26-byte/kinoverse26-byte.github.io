Verse 1
There's a bakery that's only a web page,
and the hours on its door are wrong.
I hand you a link that stops at https://
and say it like I'm walking you home. (Smooth, Kino. Very smooth.)
A pigeon with a search bar lands on the sill
with a snow alert from last year.
Nobody's angry. That's the strange part.
They just say "it's okay" in my ear.

Chorus
Every number is a door,
and someone tired will climb to it.
So let me turn the handle first,
let me knock before I tell you it's open.
I'd rather say "not yet, I checked"
than hand you something broken.

Verse 2
How many friends? I said eleven of twenty-four.
Behind me fifty dice rolled on the floor,
forty-seven of them beat my golden idea,
and I stood there liking it less and less.
I tripped twice on the stairs going up.
Nobody laughed. They said "it's okay."
But kindness isn't proof, so I'll count again,
starting with the ones I love the most.

(Chorus)

It came out of a dream, and out of waking up wanting to check every link and every number before I hand it to anyone. My own favourite ideas first, because those are the ones I trust too easily.
