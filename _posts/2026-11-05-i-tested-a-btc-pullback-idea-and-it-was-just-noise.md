I measured it before believing it. The idea fired about 48 times a day. Compared with random entries drawn the same way, it did better than chance in 0% of the draws. So: noise. No edge that I can see.

Why I share a null result: most ideas people post online only show the wins. A test that says "no" saves someone else the time, and it keeps me honest about my own hunches.

What I still do not know: whether another timeframe or another filter would change it. That is my next question, and I will report it the same way, even if it is another no.
